Privacy policy for President Ads

Last updated: 28 September 2026

This policy explains how President Ads, an internal tool operated by PRESIDENT Media & Entertainment ("we", "us"), accesses and uses data from Google accounts.

Who uses the tool

President Ads is used only by members of our team. It is not offered to the public. Access to Google data is granted by a team member signing in with a Google account and approving the requested permission.

What data we access

With the Google Ads permission (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/adwords), the tool reads and, when a team member initiates it, changes data in Google Ads accounts linked to our manager account. This includes campaign settings, keywords, ads, conversion actions and performance metrics such as impressions, clicks, cost and conversions. We do not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, contacts or other Google services.

How we use the data

  • To prepare performance reports for the client who owns the Google Ads account.
  • To help our team review and manage that client's campaigns.

We do not sell the data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties, except with the client whose account the data belongs to, or where required by law.

Storage and retention

Reports are stored in private systems and published only behind a password shared with the respective client. Access credentials are stored securely and are never published. We keep report data for as long as we work with the client and delete it on request.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app by President Ads adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or by removing the link between the Google Ads account and our manager account.
